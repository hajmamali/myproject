# Architecture Review Template

## Purpose
Provide a reusable structure for reviewing architectural changes.

## Required Fields
- [CHANGE]
- [REASON]
- [SCOPE]
- [RISK]
- [VALIDATION]
- [ROLLBACK]

## Review Questions
- Does this change preserve the shared architecture?
- Does it introduce unnecessary coupling or duplication?
- What is the expected impact on maintainability and future evolution?

## Expected Output
A concise review that documents the architectural implications and the decision basis.
