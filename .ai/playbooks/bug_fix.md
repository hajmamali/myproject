# Bug Fix Playbook

## When To Use
Use this playbook when addressing a defect, failing behavior, or observed incorrect outcome.

## Required Steps
1. Reproduce or understand the issue.
2. Trace the relevant logic and impacted boundaries.
3. Identify the root cause before changing implementation.
4. Apply the smallest correct fix.
5. Validate the fix and document the behavior change.

## Validation Checklist
- The fix addresses the root cause rather than the symptom.
- Existing behavior remains intact where appropriate.
- The change is documented and traceable.

## Expected Output
A precise fix with evidence of resolution and no avoidable regressions.
