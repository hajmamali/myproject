# Architecture Decision Record Template

## Purpose
Provide a reusable template for capturing significant architecture and governance decisions.

## Scope
Use this template when making changes that affect structure, interfaces, policy, or long-term maintainability.

## Required Sections
- Title
- Status
- Context
- Decision
- Reason
- Alternatives Considered
- Risks
- Validation
- Rollback Plan

## Rules
- Record the decision when it is made, not after the fact.
- Capture rationale and trade-offs explicitly.
- Keep the record concise and reviewable.

## Expected Behavior
Future contributors should be able to understand why a decision was made and what alternatives were considered.

## Anti-Patterns
- Storing decisions only in informal discussion threads.
- Omitting rationale or consequences.

## Quality Criteria
The ADR should be clear, durable, and useful for future reasoning.
