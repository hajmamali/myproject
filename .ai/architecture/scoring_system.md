# Scoring System

## Purpose
Describe how architectural and governance findings can be ranked, compared, and prioritized.

## Responsibilities
- Provide a consistent basis for evaluating risk, severity, and impact.
- Support triage and prioritization for governance actions.
- Improve clarity in reporting and decision-making.

## Data Flow
Scores are derived from evidence, rule outcomes, and architectural context.

## Boundaries
The scoring system should remain a decision-support mechanism, not a substitute for judgment.

## Interfaces
It should expose clear categories and rationale for each score.

## Failure Modes
- Overly simplistic scoring that hides nuance.
- Unclear weighting of risk factors.
- False confidence produced by opaque metrics.

## Future Evolution
The scoring model should evolve as the governance system matures and becomes more evidence-driven.
